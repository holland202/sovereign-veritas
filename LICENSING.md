## Code contributions and independent reports are different

- **Code proposed for this repository:** A submitter should have authority to offer the proposed contribution under the existing MIT repository license and should preserve third-party notices. Contributors normally retain their underlying copyright; this document requires no assignment or contributor license agreement. Any nonstandard terms must be assessed before merge.
- **Independently authored reviews and reports:** Do not infer an assignment, broad sublicensing permission, commercial endorsement or joint authorship solely because the author allowed publication. Record the specific document/revision, author-approved credit, relevant publication permission and provenance. If a review includes separate licensing terms, check them. A permission to publish in this repository does not by itself prove permission for unrelated republication or marketing.
- **External code, datasets, images, model weights and excerpts:** Maintain upstream notices and follow the applicable permissions. A root MIT notice is not evidence that all third-party rights were transferred. When existing MIT distribution and documented author-specific terms appear inconsistent, flag them for rights review; do not rewrite history or assert a silent exception.

This page does not impose a new license on that review or rewrite its author-approved credit.

## Potential commercial work

An owner of the necessary rights may develop separately licensed **new** components, hosted services, consulting or custom integrations. Those future arrangements must not be represented as limitations on existing MIT-licensed material. Before licensing improvements authored with others, resolve the actual contributor rights.

## Participation and review

Participation in community research is voluntary. Where a particular research collaboration is to be unpaid, time expectations and publication conditions should be agreed explicitly; any paid professional evaluation requires a separate written arrangement. An independent reviewer may report negative or inconclusive findings; permission to reproduce a test is not an endorsement of the system.
