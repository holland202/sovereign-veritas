## What I'd most like challenged
1. **Order and risk.** W3 changes the contract digest that the public challenge and Amos's baseline cite. Should W3 go last, or become its own release?
2. **W1 cost.** Requiring signatures breaks the unsigned quick start and some tests. Is an explicit `--allow-unsigned` the right escape hatch, or should development use a throwaway key?
3. **W3 semantics.** "Cannot be the only support": does that mean all three runtime fields DEFAULTED gives DEFER, or *any* DEFAULTED field gives DEFER? These differ a lot (FACTS: make_package marks two fields DEFAULTED by default). Your acceptance cases will have to pick one.
